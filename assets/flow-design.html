Long BeardDesign, one step at a time. Each step writes one file and waits for your yes.

1Brief

The questions a designer asks before looking.

answer once, correct the read-back.

2Board

Your references at full size, with each product's other screens. A big board is grouped by look.

lead, like, leave out, and say why.

3Direction

Eight first screens, each a reference you liked adapted to your product, never copied (your lead gets two). One is recommended by the brief.

pick, mix or ask for more; confirm the principles, then see it on a phone and your hardest screen.

4Identity

Type and colour from the chosen screen, with close options on it.

pick, refine once.

5Prototype

Every key screen and state in the chosen screen's frame, seen as one set. Layout and motion options sit on the real page, with a switcher.

click through and approve. DESIGN.md is written.

6Figma

Tokens, components, and every screen with its states.

mark each frame Ready. Build starts from there.

Your taste picks: the references, the screen, each part, the type, colours and motion.
The agent does the craft: it adapts your references to your product and adds nothing that isn't on your board.
Docs stay clean: committed files describe the style, never a source.
