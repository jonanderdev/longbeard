1design-brief 1 message

A studio's questions: first screen in five seconds, personality, feel and never, apart from, what is fixed. You answer; one read-back.

docs/design/design-brief.md

2design-moodboard 1–2 picks

Your board read and its gaps filled, or a wide search. You mark like or no on a picker; each keep gets its move in words.

design-moodboard.md

3design-direction the direction

3–4 pictures of your subject, then eight first screens, each one of your references rebuilt with your content and changed by a move or two borrowed from others. You pick, ask for more, mix. Then a light touch-up: contrast, anything missing, never the composition.

design-direction.md

4design-identity 3 stops

The chosen screen's type and colours, with close options rendered on it. You pick, refine once, and approve the identity board.

design-identity.md + .html

5design-prototype you approve

The key screens and their states, in one go, with options only where a layout forks. You approve, then DESIGN.md.

DESIGN.md

6design-figma

Tokens, the identity board on the Cover, components, every screen and state, checked by design-check. You mark each Ready.
