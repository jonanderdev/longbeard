# Design brief: Ada Lund, pet portraits

- takeaway: "she sees my dog the way I do"; the one thing they do is book a session
- first seen on: desktop
- pace: full
- words: warm, observant, unfussy · never cute
- personality: loud 3 · picture-led 5 · rich 2 · cool 2 · playful 2 · new 4 · dense 2 (each 1 to 5, 5 the named end: loud 2 is fairly quiet; one left out was not answered)
- feel: like a photo book on a coffee table: big pictures, a name, a line of text
- never: paw-print clip art; pastel pet-shop colours; studio backdrops; a template site with a picture slider, where people only ever sent a message instead of booking
- apart from: pet-shop and vet-clinic sites, and studio pet photographers with props and backdrops · distance 4
- fixed: a new brand and a new name; no colours, faces or logo owned or refused; light only; pictures: theirs (the ones in the moodboard folder are stand-ins until theirs are swapped in); English
- accessibility: AA
- motion: quiet · driven by scroll · character smooth, on everything
- voice: plain, warm and first-person, the way Ada would say it to an owner at the door: short sentences about the animal, never baby talk, e.g. "Two hours at your place, on the sofa where she always sleeps. I'll take the pictures; you keep her busy."
- decides: only them
