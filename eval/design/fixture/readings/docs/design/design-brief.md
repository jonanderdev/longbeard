# Design brief: Hearth Bakehouse

- takeaway: a real neighbourhood bakery, and today's bread is still there; the one thing they do is order a loaf for pickup
- first seen on: desktop
- pace: full
- words: warm · honest · exact · never fussy
- personality: loud 2 · picture-led 4 · rich 2 · cool 1 · playful 2 · new 4 · dense 2 (each 1 to 5, 5 the named end: loud 2 is fairly quiet)
- feel: like the shop at 7am: flour in the air, the chalkboard
- never: glossy chain-store; stock-photo smiles; a template site with a carousel, where nobody found the order button
- apart from: supermarket bakery counters and food-delivery apps · distance 4 (1 familiar to its users, 5 nothing like the category)
- fixed: a new brand, nothing owned or refused; light only; pictures: none yet; English
- accessibility: AA
- motion: quiet · driven by time · character smooth, on everything
- voice: plain and neighbourly, like the baker talking across the counter at 7am: short sentences, real numbers, no exclamation marks, e.g. "Six seeded ryes left for tomorrow. Pick yours up from 7:30."
- decides: only them
