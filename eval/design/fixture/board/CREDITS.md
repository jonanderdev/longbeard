# The eval board

Ten pictures of web pages and posters, made for this eval. Each takes its composition, type, spacing and colour from a starred card on a private art-direction board, rebuilt here with our own words, fictional names and the photographs below. No original design or image is included, and the code that drew them is not kept: these JPEGs are the fixture.
