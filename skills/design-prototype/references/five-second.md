# The five-second check

Read by `design-direction` (the chosen screen, after the touch-up) and `design-prototype` (the first screen and each key screen, before `DESIGN.md`). The design brief's takeaway says what someone should think and do in five seconds. This checks it on eyes that know nothing about the product. Every other check asks whether the screen follows the rules; this one asks whether it works.

## With fresh agents

Three agents that have seen nothing: a new agent or a clean session each, given only the capture (`capture.mjs … --clip 0,0,1440,900`, or the device size for an app; for a site most people open on their phone, the 390 capture too). Never the brief, the design brief, the product's name from anywhere but the screen, or this conversation. Each gets exactly:

> You glance at this screen for five seconds. Answer in one short line each, from what you see, not what you guess:
> 1. What is this?
> 2. Who is it for?
> 3. What would you do first?
> 4. One word for how it feels.

No agent can be started clean (no subagents, no second session): say "five-second check: not run" and offer the people version. Never answer the questions yourself: you know too much.

## Compare, as facts

One table: the takeaway's think and do, then each agent's answers, then a verdict per row.

| | Takeaway | 1 | 2 | 3 | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | a bakery, open now | … | … | … | 3 of 3 |
| What to do | order for pickup | … | … | … | 1 of 3 |
| Feel | warm, plain, exact (never: fussy) | … | … | … | 2 of 3 |

- **Got it:** two or three of three say it in any words.
- **Missed:** one or none. Say what they read instead ("two read it as a café menu, not an order page").
- **Feel:** compared with the design brief's three words and its never word. A never word in an answer is said, always.

## Then

A miss is a fact for them, not a redesign for you.

- **In the direction:** a miss on what to do, or what it is, is fixed only with the touch-up's own means: the headline says the takeaway outright, the primary action is present and findable, rank put right. Shown before and after, run again once. A miss only a different composition would fix is said in one line, and the choice stays theirs: keep it, or go back to the grid.
- **In the prototype:** the same on each key screen, where "what to do" is that screen's own job (a basket: check out). Fixed inside the identity and the direction.
- **The feel** is never fixed here. A never word in the answers is said with the screens; changing the look is `design-identity` or the direction again, on their word.

## People, offered once

Agents read a screen like a careful stranger, not like the people the product is for. Offer once, with the result:

> Want a real check? Show the screen for five seconds to two or three people who might use it, and ask the same four questions. Paste what they say.

Their answers outrank the agents'. Record them the same way. A no stands.
