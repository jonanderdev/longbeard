# A graphic

Read when the idea is a piece to look at, not a product to use: a poster, a card or invitation, a social post, a cover. Everything in `SKILL.md` holds except what is said here. A piece counts as a screen wherever a skill says screen.

The kind is said once, here, on the brief's `surface` line, and no later skill asks it again.

## What changes

- **Do not ask for flows or key screens.** A piece has none.
- **Ask for four things:** what it is for, who sees it and where, the facts it must carry, and the format.
- **One round, two at most.** Start with what the idea gives and recommend the rest. A birthday card needs a few lines, not a page.
- **Facts first.** The facts are what, when, where, who, and a link or a code if there is one. Each is given, or marked missing. A missing fact is never invented: it is written "missing" and stays in the brief until they supply it.
- **Format.** A size (a paper size, or pixels), portrait or landscape, print or screen. For a set (a poster with a story and a square version), list each piece with its size. A single piece is one line.
- **A set or a single piece.** Ask only if the idea does not say.

## The brief

About 250 words. The headings the design skills read by name stay; flows, key screens and v1 are replaced.

```markdown
# <Piece name>

<One sentence: what it is, and for whom.>

## What it is for
<The one thing it makes someone do or feel, in two sentences. The cost of it going unseen.>

## Seen by
<Who sees it, where, and from how far: a street, a phone feed, a hand.>

## Must carry
- what: <…>
- when: <…, or "missing">
- where: <…, or "missing">
- who: <…>
- link or code: <…, or "none">

## Format
- <size, portrait or landscape, print or screen>
- <for a set, one line per piece>

## Surface
graphic. <One line why: a piece to look at, not a tool to use.>

## Constraints
- <What is fixed: a deadline, print or screen, one colour or full colour, a logo or colours they own. Not tech choices.>

## Position
- against: <what pieces of its kind usually look like, or "none: a personal piece">
- remembered for: <one concrete thing someone would describe to a friend>
- personality: <three adjectives>. Says: "<a line it would say>". Never: "<a line it would never say>"

## Identity nouns
<4–8 concrete nouns from its world.>

## Out of scope
- <What it will not do or carry.>
```

Every section has content. "None" is an answer. A guess is not. The brief names no look, as in any brief.

## After writing

Offer `design` in one line. A graphic's run ends at its exported Figma frame: never offer Architecture or Plan, even though no `GLOSSARY.md` exists.
